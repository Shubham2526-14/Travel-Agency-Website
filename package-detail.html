Experience the speed of Japan, fast and slow, from Tokyo to Osaka

Japan is a country of contrasts — bullet trains that glide past snow-capped mountains at 300 km/h, and quiet backstreets where time seems to stand still. This nine-day journey takes you from the electric energy of Tokyo to the laid-back charm of Osaka, with stops along the way that show you both sides of modern Japan.

Ride the Shinkansen through the countryside, wander the temples of Kyoto at golden hour, and sit down to a home-cooked meal with a local family in a small mountain village. You'll explore ancient shrines, bustling markets, and neon-lit streets — all connected by some of the fastest, most reliable rail network in the world.

This trip is built for travelers who want to see it all without rushing — a balance of guided experiences and free time to explore at your own pace. Whether it's your first trip to Japan or your fifth, there's always something new to discover between Tokyo and Osaka.

Trip Itinerary

Konnichiwa! Welcome to Japan. Your adventure starts with a welcome meeting at 6 pm tonight in Tokyo. Bursting with contemporary urban culture, Tokyo has fascinating museums, world-class shopping and backstreets stuffed with restaurants and karaoke bars. If you arrive early, maybe check out an Urban Adventure Tour in Tokyo or walk down Shinjuku's Omoide Tokocho (Memory Lane) – a crowded alley of busy restaurants and bar stalls that's now one of the best spots to try some of Tokyo's local fast food. After your meeting, follow your leader on an orientation walk to familiarize yourself with the neighborhood and surroundings near the hotel , then maybe head out for dinner and get to know your fellow travellers.

Please carry the following original documents for the trip

A copy of your travel insurance policy covering the full duration of the trip.

Name and phone number of someone we can reach in case of an emergency.

A valid tourist visa for Japan, applied for in advance of your travel date.
